Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and protect the information you share while using BillPro to run smarter, faster billing operations.

Updated August 2026
  • Transparent data handling
  • Secure account practices
  • Responsible business data usage

1. Introduction

Welcome to BillPro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our billing and business management platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use BillPro, we may collect information such as:

  • Company or organisation name
  • Administrator name
  • Business email address
  • Mobile number
  • GST information
  • Account and login information
  • Billing and invoice information

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to provide and improve BillPro services, manage your account, generate invoices, calculate applicable taxes, provide business reports, and maintain platform security.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Business Data

Information entered into BillPro, including customers, invoices, products, payments, and tax information, is used to provide the billing services requested by you.

6. Cookies and Technology

BillPro may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand how the platform is being used.

7. Third-Party Services

Some BillPro functionality may depend on third-party services. Information shared with such services will be handled according to their applicable privacy policies.

8. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for ensuring that the information entered into your BillPro account is accurate and lawful.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact the BillPro support team.
